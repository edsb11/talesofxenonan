A lone rider crossed a rainy forest. It was dark and cloudy even though the light hadn’t gone. The man wore straw puffs on his neck and arms, and an old brown coat tainted, scratched and stained by time. His head was a dark cloud of puffing black smoke, while his eyes shone white as the moons in the sky.

  

He was tired, grabbing the horse guide so he didn’t fall from it. The saddle was old like him, and changed as he was.

  

After crossing some trees, he found a pole stuck in the ground, with a lantern on its edge. 

  

There was also a scarred sign there, it said “Bertha”, while a dirty road followed behind it. A thunder struck like a gun in the skies.

  

He grabs the big stick and uses his finger to light fire in the lantern.

  

A dark spark quickly burst into blue flames, which illuminated the surroundings.

  

He kept going with his horse, until his silhouette fades in the water fog.

  

. . .

From the dark, the Sigil of Hollow is shown in a yellow glow. Its shape was a triangle with something that reminds you of wings on each side of it.

  

[A Deep old man 's voice] “Nikolai Telamon was his name before this madness. Nobody knew what he was doing, truly. Some liked him as well as some disliked.”

  

“He became Hollow, from the inside to the outside.”

  

...

  

Birds chirped in the tall trees.

  

Sunrays bathed the lands in ease.

  

In the ground of a crop some weird worms struggled to move while lots of vines were swirling in and out from the ground. The worms moved and left green vine wires all around.

  

Suddenly, a furry paw steps and kills some of the worms. A black cat.

  

The cat ran so fast and its paw smashed the ground so hard it seemed the little beast was flying.

  

It had big yellow eyes as glass globes, skimming through every place in front of him.

  

Behind the cat, a boy with sandy hair ran after it. The boy was strangely wearing white sailor suits, and holding its hat so it wouldn’t fall from his head.

  

The boy laughed and scared away the birds on the way.

  

[FR]: Surrender now!!! [Laughs hysterically]

  

The cat jumped through fences and so the boy, the cat ran inside bushes and the boy jumped over. They were chasing each other for very long.

  

They ran toward an orchard and the cat ran towards a big old wooden house at the end of it.

  

The boy felt in wonderment looking around. 

[FR]: Ooooh...

  

The orchard was Filled with cherry trees where the boy picked up as much as he deserved, staining his clothes and mouth with red.

  

He spent some minutes there picking up, almost forgetting about the cat.

  

He fell from a small tree and remembered what he was doing.

  

[FR]: Oh kitty!

  

He raised and looked at the house. It was dark and the planks were eaten away. He could hear crying and echoing meows from the inside.

  

The boy picked up a little stone ax from his belt and prepared for any imminent combat.

  

He was brave, a brave boy, he was Fitzroy!

  

In the dark, while Fitzroy stepped in the old cobblestone floor, the cat stopped moaning.

  

[FR]: Hey... Now it’s time to surrender, outlaw!

  

He kept walking, looking in every direction without seeing anything.

  

Spinning and swirling with the ax in his hand, he bumps on something and everything falls out in his front.

  

It sounded like a bunch of rocks and sticks falling together.

  

But then…

  

Footsteps. The boy hears footsteps coming from somewhere in the hall.

  

It was a shadow. A small humanoid shadow with thick arms and chest. 

  

In every move the shadow performed, the boy could hear grass sounds.

  

He was terrified. 

  

[FR]: Monster! [Screams]

  

The shadow jumps too with the boy.

  

[MB]: Monster? Monster! Where!? Run away!

  

[FR]: Monster! Monster!

  

The cat screams from the boy’s back, and he accidentally steps on the cat’s tail, making it run away from there.

  

[FR]: No! You! Come back here!

  

The boy runs away from the screaming shadow. He cannot see anything and starts bumping on one wall, then the second, and many others to come.

  

Many things fell. Glass was shattering and wood furniture falling.

  

The boy crossed a door frame and saw the two illuminating eyes of that weird cat at the end of a long corridor, and decided to follow.

  

He looked back and he saw the shadow at the main hall. It looked like a moving bush.

  

[MB]: I’m not a monster! - It cried like an old lady.

  

The boy had goosebumps with the horrific cry and ran away where the cat was.

  

The cat knew where to go. It went right and left, crossing many corridors of that big wooden mansion while a thunder sang outside.

  

The boy was following, now panting, but still with the ax in his hand.

  

[FR]: Where are you going, skunk!?

  

The cat looked back for a second and the boy could see his glowing eyes from very close.

  

Then, the cat started climbing the wooden walls towards an open hole in the ceiling. Rain came from the hole.

  

[FR]: Hey, where are you going!? No!! Don’t leave me here!

  

The crying came reverberating from every hall in the mansion. The boy held his hat and sat on the ground, protecting himself from anything above.

  

Up there, the soaked and curious cat walked through many roof holes and looked down, he found many places of that abandoned home.

  

He found the hole where that other creature was crying out loud, and what he saw was strange.

  

The creature was entirely made up of grass but it had bony hands as a skeleton. Their arms were covered by vine leaves and straw bushes. It was the weirdest thing the little eyes had ever seen.

  

The head, a skull. A humanoid skull with a shining sigil glowing in its forehead. 

  

The creature down there cried and grabbed what it let fall on the floor: A bunch of pumpkins.

  

Yes, the things they let it fall were big pumpkins the size of the boy’s head, almost.

  

The creature picked up one and put it like armor, first the chest, then the head. And finally, a straw hat with golden pigtails on each side. It looked like a Halloween costume.

  

The face was carved and it looked more friendly than how the creature looked before.

  

But it still cried, and followed the path where the boy was.

  

The cat walked back from the hole it came climbing and looked down. It saw the boy, shaking in fear, and the creature there, at the door.

  

The boy screamed once again, but so loud the cat’s ears were shaking.

  

[FR]: I swear!... I swear I'm gonna be nice next time. I’m not gonna run after anything in my life! PLEASE, PLEASE DON’T HURT ME!!

  

The thing stumbled towards the boy while he was paralyzed. It raised its hand, and he could see.

  

The carved face of a pumpkin on its head, looking at him with empty eyes.

  

[MB][With a sharp voice of an old woman]: Who… You… Be…?

  

[FR][Absolutely shaking]: I... I… I’m Fitzroy! And… You…?

  

[MB]: You call me Miss Burges.

  

[FR]: S-Sorry for… This…

  

And she started screaming like an angry grandmother.

  

[MB]: Don’t come home ever again! You bad bad bad child!

  

[FR]: I’m sorry, ow! I’m sorry!

  

She started picking up on his ear and lifting him up, and Fitzroy felt her bony fingers.

  

[MB]: You call old woman monster! Who taught that to child!?

  

[FR]: Aaaaaaargh!!

  

She was dragging him by the ear and walked away from the small room.

  

The cat jumped back down, and curiously followed them.

  

[MB]: What your name, child!?

  

[FR]: Ow, Fitzroy! I already told ya!

  

[MB]: Lousy child clean up everything!

  

And she released him, tossing a broom and a bucket to Fitzroy.

  

[MB]: You put the broken things in bucket, and clean dust everywhere!

  

Fitzroy held the broom, and clenched his fists on the stick.

  

[FR]: You’re not my grandma! You can’t tell me to do things [Showing up his tongue]

  

Then the little black cat came along and rubbed himself in Miss Burges’ straw-made legs, meowing.

  

[FR]: Hey, granny. Is this your cat?

  

[MB]: Yes! My cat it is!

  

And she petted the little cat and played with it. And when she noticed Fitzroy was looking, she screamed “GO CLEAN STUFF!”

  

He grabbed the broom and began to pick up shattered pottery and put it on the bucket.

  

. . .

  

Rotten hands shoved fingers in the dirt. It was a grave, the mossy tombstone of a forgotten name.

  

It was dug to reveal a skull only. The hand then raised two fingers and emitted blue fire.

  

It touched the forehead of that skull and the Sigil burned in. The skeleton started moving.

  

Above, the person who touched the skeleton and watched the struggle of that bony body was Nikolai Telamon.

  

He raised his rotten hand to help that person, and raised the other to give them a wobbling worm.

Both of them were shadows under the storm. The skeleton picked up the worm and…

  

The worm quickly moved through their bodies, and the skeleton screamed!

  

The worm jumped here and there, leaving their vines along the skeleton body.

  

In the center of the skeleton’s chest, a brown heart made out of branches was pounding. And arms were being formed like tendons and veins. 

  

Then, the thing who was once a skeleton was panting like it relearned how to breathe.

  

Nikolai raised a pumpkin, and a knife. He started carving it like a Jack o’ lantern.

  

When he was finished, he tossed the seeds away and put the carved pumpkin on the creature’s head.

  

[NT][With a deep hoarse voice]: Welcome. 

  

. . .

  

There was a picture frame over a bookcase of a family wearing old Victorian clothing. The picture was fading, and Fitzroy picked it up to look closer.

  

There was a man wearing a top hat and smiling with hands in his coat pockets. On his side, a sympathetic lady with a long dress and a little cat on her arms. It was a white cat with dark eyes.

  

Fitzroy found it weird, but couldn’t let his eyes drive away from the picture.

  

Miss Burges adjusted her great golden victrola to play songs that were never once heard by the boy. Songs from another age.

  

Candles were lit, and the cat played with dust balls as Miss Burges watched it. She wasn’t looking at the boy while he was cleaning.

And that was good. Fitzroy was too lazy to do anything, and was just sitting on the floor with his head resting on a couch seat, looking at the picture.

  

After a while, he put the picture away and started working again.

  

But then, when he raised from the floor, the lady noticed him…

  

[MB]: Child. Is it not too soon to come?

  

Fitzroy stared.

  

[FR]: Well… I was supposed to be home now. I think it’s late for me, already.

  

She looked down for a few seconds, then she looked up.

  

[MB]: The picture. It is me. It is my husband. It is my cat.

  

Fitzroy looked at her, and the cat. But something was missing… Her husband?

  

[FR]: Then why… Why are you wearing this? Am I not supposed to see your face?

  

[MB]: No… You not supposed here.

  

The cat jumped between couches and looked at the boy while Miss Burges talked.

  

[MB]: You can come here when you done with everything. Everything.

  

[FR]: I’m not… I don’t understand, miss.

  

Then Fitzroy grabbed the broom and started cleaning again, frustrated.

  

While that, the lady kept talking.

  

[MB]: That common here, child. People here, they come after everything set for them.

  

Fitzroy grabbed another bucket and tossed more shards inside.

  

[MB]: I, me, myself, found good life here. Better than before.

  

[FR]: You lived in another city? 

  

[MB]: Another life. Yes. Another time. Another world.

  

Something made Fitzroy’s grip stronger on the broom. It wasn’t fear, but it was unsettling.

  

The song behind his ears was flaky. A crying violin made the light of the candles warmer.

  

But suddenly, the door knocked.

  

Both of them looked, unprepared.

  

[FR]: Is that… Your husband?

  

But then, the person on the other side was impatient. They opened the door abruptly, and Fitzroy was so scared he jumped backwards to hide, and so the cat did.

  

Nikolai Telamon, the Hollow. He was taller as the door frame when he opened the front door.

  

[MB]: Knock before open, gnome! Oh… OH! Oh… I’m sorry, I’m sorry!
